# Claim and authority boundary

Report each layer independently: structural validity, digest/reference integrity, conformance
profile result, residual state, and authority decision. A core result omits operational checks.
Operational authority is deny-by-default: stale, revoked, unknown, quarantined, denied, and
residualized states do not authorize an action. A passing check speaks only to the declared
input and procedure, not to truth outside them or to verifier independence unless that was audited.
